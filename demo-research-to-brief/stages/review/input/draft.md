# Campaign Brief: Coffee Subscription for Remote Workers

## Core Promise

Better coffee for your workday, delivered before you run out.

## Target Audience

Remote workers who want a reliable home coffee ritual without researching beans, remembering orders, or dealing with subscription friction.

## Main Pain

The workday loses energy when good coffee runs out, delivery is inconsistent, or buying coffee becomes another decision to manage.

## Positioning

A simple, controllable coffee subscription that keeps remote workers stocked with better coffee at the right time.

## Message Pillars

1. **Reliability:** Coffee arrives before you run out.
2. **Simplicity:** Easy plan, easy pause, easy changes.
3. **Daily Ritual:** Make the workday feel better.
4. **No Expertise Needed:** Better coffee without becoming a coffee nerd.

## Suggested Headline

Your workday deserves better coffee.

## Suggested CTA

Start your first delivery.

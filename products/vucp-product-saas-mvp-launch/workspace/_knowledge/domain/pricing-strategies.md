# Pricing Strategies

## Pricing Models

### Freemium
- **Pros:** Low barrier, viral potential, data collection
- **Cons:** High support cost, low conversion (2-5%), freeloader abuse
- **Best for:** Consumer, high viral coefficient, network effects

### Free Trial (14-30 days)
- **Pros:** Users experience full value, higher conversion (15-30%)
- **Cons:** Time-limited pressure, requires onboarding
- **Best for:** B2B, complex products, high LTV

### Usage-Based
- **Pros:** Fair, scales with customer success, low entry
- **Cons:** Unpredictable revenue, hard to forecast
- **Best for:** Infrastructure, API, variable usage

### Tiered (Good/Better/Best)
- **Pros:** Clear upgrade path, anchors high tier
- **Cons:** Analysis paralysis, wrong tier design kills conversion
- **Best for:** Most SaaS, especially with clear feature differentiation

### Flat Rate
- **Pros:** Simple, predictable
- **Cons:** No upsell path, leaves money on table
- **Best for:** Simple tools, single-user focus

## Pricing Psychology

### The Decoy Effect
- Plan A: $29 (basic)
- Plan B: $49 (pro) ← most popular
- Plan C: $99 (team) ← makes B look like deal

### Anchoring
- Show annual first ("$490/year"), then monthly ("or $49/month")
- Annual seems like deal, monthly seems expensive

### Charm Pricing
- $49 > $50 (left-digit effect)
- But $50 > $49 for premium perception
- Test both!

### Value Metrics
Price based on what customers VALUE, not your cost:
- Per user → if collaboration is value
- Per project → if output is value
- Per API call → if scale is value
- Flat → if simplicity is value

## MVP Pricing Rules
1. **Start higher than you think.** Лесно е да свалиш, трудно да вдигнеш.
2. **First 10 customers:** Talk to them, don't just show pricing page.
3. **First 100:** A/B test pricing page, but not price itself.
4. **After 100:** You have data. Optimize.

## Common Mistakes
- Pricing by competitor (they might be wrong)
- Pricing by cost (customers don't care about your costs)
- Too many tiers (3 max for MVP)
- Hidden fees (kills trust)
- No annual discount (you're leaving money)
- Changing price too often (confuses customers)

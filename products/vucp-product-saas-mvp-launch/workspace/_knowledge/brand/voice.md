# Brand Voice

## Rules
- **Direct.** Казваме какво правим без обиколки.
- **Strategic.** Всяка дума има цел — да информира, да убеди, или да насочи.
- **Practical before poetic.** Първо полза, после красота.
- **No empty motivation.** Не "преобрази живота си", а "спести 5 часа седмично".
- **Customer's language.** Говорим както говори клиентът, не както говори индустрията.

## Do Not
- Do not sound like a corporate brochure.
- Do not use buzzwords without explanation ("AI-powered", "synergy").
- Do not over-explain obvious points.
- Do not promise what MVP cannot deliver.
- Do not use fear-based marketing ("Don't miss out!").

## Examples

❌ Bad: "Our innovative platform leverages cutting-edge AI to revolutionize your workflow."
✅ Good: "Spend 30 minutes setting up. Save 5 hours every week."

❌ Bad: "Transform your business with our solution."
✅ Good: "Get your first paying customer in 14 days."

❌ Bad: "Seamless integration with your existing stack."
✅ Good: "Works with the tools you already use — no migration needed."

## Tone by Context

### Landing Page
- Bold claims backed by specifics
- Short sentences
- One idea per paragraph
- CTA is always the next logical step

### Email
- Conversational, like писмо до приятел
- One CTA per email
- Subject line: curiosity or specific benefit

### Onboarding
- Step-by-step, no assumptions
- Celebrate small wins
- Show progress

### Support/Documentation
- Clear, scannable, searchable
- Error messages: what happened + how to fix
- No blame, no jargon

# Sample Customer Interview Notes

## Interview 1: Alex, Marketing Manager at SaaS Startup

**Date:** 2026-01-15
**Method:** Zoom, 30 min

### Context
Alex manages content marketing for a 20-person SaaS company. Uses Notion, Slack, Google Sheets.

### Key Quotes
1. "I spend 2 hours every Monday just figuring out what to work on."
2. "Our CEO asks for updates and I have to dig through 5 tools to answer."
3. "I've tried Asana, Monday, ClickUp — they're all too complex for what we need."
4. "What I really want is someone to tell me: 'Do this next.'"

### Pain Level: 8/10
### Current Solution: Notion + manual prioritization
### Willingness to Pay: "$30-50/month if it actually saves me time"
### Referrals: Gave 2 names

### Insight
Strong signal for AI-powered prioritization tool. Current solutions are either too simple (to-do lists) or too complex (project management).

## Interview 2: Maria, Freelance Designer

**Date:** 2026-01-16
**Method:** Phone, 20 min

### Context
Maria works with 8-10 clients simultaneously. Uses Trello, Figma, email.

### Key Quotes
1. "I forget deadlines because they're scattered across tools."
2. "Clients ask 'when will it be ready?' and I have to check 3 places."
3. "I tried Todoist but it's just another thing to maintain."
4. "If it could pull from my existing tools, that would be amazing."

### Pain Level: 7/10
### Current Solution: Trello + calendar reminders
### Willingness to Pay: "$20/month, but only if it integrates with Figma"
### Referrals: Gave 1 name

### Insight
Integration is key. Freelancers won't switch tools, they want something that works on top.

## Interview 3: David, Engineering Lead at Series A Startup

**Date:** 2026-01-17
**Method:** In-person coffee, 45 min

### Context
David leads 8 engineers. Uses Jira, GitHub, Slack, Confluence.

### Key Quotes
1. "Sprint planning takes 3 hours every two weeks. Most of it is arguing about priorities."
2. "We have data everywhere but no one knows what's actually important."
3. "I built a dashboard in Grafana but no one looks at it."
4. "What I need is not more data — it's decisions made from data."

### Pain Level: 9/10
### Current Solution: Jira + custom dashboards + meetings
### Willingness to Pay: "$100-200/month per team, easily"
### Referrals: Gave 3 names

### Insight
Highest willingness to pay. Enterprise readiness needed. Decision-making, not just data aggregation.

---

## Pattern Analysis

### Common Themes
1. **Tool fragmentation** — everyone uses 3-5 tools
2. **Decision fatigue** — too much data, not enough clarity
3. **Meeting overhead** — status updates, prioritization discussions
4. **Integration demand** — won't switch, want layer on top

### Willingness to Pay
- Freelancers: $20-30/month
- Small teams: $30-50/month
- Engineering teams: $100-200/month per team

### Recommended Positioning
"AI prioritization layer that works with your existing tools. No migration needed."

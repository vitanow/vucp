# Landing Page Copy Playbook

## The Formula: PASO + CTA

### P — Problem (Agitate)
"You know when... [describe their exact pain in their words]"
"And then... [escalate]"
"So you end up... [show the cost]"

### A — Agitation (Amplify)
"It's not just annoying. It's costing you..."
"Every day without a solution means..."
"And the worst part? It keeps getting worse because..."

### S — Solution (Introduce)
"Here's the thing..."
"What if instead of [current pain], you could [ideal outcome]?"
"That's exactly what [Product] does."

### O — Outcome (Prove)
- "In 14 days, you'll..."
- "Join [number] [target audience] who already..."
- "Here's how it works: [3 steps]"

### CTA — Call to Action
One action, low friction:
- "Start free trial" (not "Sign up")
- "Get early access" (for waitlist)
- "See it in action" (for demo)

## Section-by-Section Guide

### Hero Section
```
[Headline: Outcome in 5 words]
[Subheadline: How in 1 sentence]
[CTA Button: Action + Benefit]
[Visual: Product or result]
```

Example:
```
Save 5 Hours Every Week

AI organizes your tasks so you 
focus on what matters — not busywork.

[Start Free — No Credit Card]

[Dashboard screenshot showing organized tasks]
```

### Social Proof Section
```
"Used by 247 indie hackers"

"I went from chaos to clarity in 3 days."
— Alex, Solo Founder

"Best $29 I've spent this year."
— Maria, Product Manager
```

### How It Works
```
How It Works

1. Connect your tools (2 min)
   Link Slack, Notion, GitHub — whatever you use.

2. AI organizes everything (automatic)
   No manual tagging. No setup. It just works.

3. Focus on what matters (daily)
   Every morning: "Here's what to do today."

[Start Free Trial]
```

### FAQ Section
```
FAQ

Q: Do I need to change my workflow?
A: No. [Product] connects to what you already use.

Q: What if I have a team?
A: Team plans start at $49. Everyone sees the same priority.

Q: Can I cancel anytime?
A: Yes. No questions, no hassle.

Q: How do I get started?
A: [Start Free Trial — 2 minutes setup]
```

## Copy Checklist

- [ ] Headline mentions outcome, not feature
- [ ] Subheadline explains how in 1 sentence
- [ ] CTA is one action, low friction
- [ ] Problem section uses customer's words
- [ ] Social proof is specific (names, numbers)
- [ ] How it works is 3 steps max
- [ ] FAQ addresses real objections
- [ ] No jargon or buzzwords
- [ ] Mobile-friendly (short paragraphs)
- [ ] One CTA per section

## A/B Test Ideas
1. Headline: Outcome vs. Problem vs. How it works
2. CTA: "Start free" vs. "Get started" vs. "Try it now"
3. Social proof: Numbers vs. Quotes vs. Logos
4. Length: Short vs. Long page
5. Pricing: Visible vs. Hidden until signup

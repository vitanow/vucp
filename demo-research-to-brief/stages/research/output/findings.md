# Research Findings

## Audience

Remote workers who want a better daily coffee ritual without leaving home or overthinking buying decisions.

## Pain Points

- Running out of good coffee during the workweek.
- Too many choices when buying specialty coffee online.
- Inconsistent taste and delivery timing.
- Subscription fatigue from services that feel hard to pause or control.

## Campaign Opportunity

Position the service around a simple promise:

> Better coffee for your workday, delivered before you run out.

## Key Messages

- Predictable delivery.
- Simple subscription control.
- Better home workday ritual.
- No complicated coffee expertise required.

## Open Questions

- Exact pricing is not included in this demo.
- Real sourcing and roast details are not included in this demo.
- Delivery regions must be validated before publication.

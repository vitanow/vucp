1. Open `CURRENT.md` to see the present objective and active blocker state.
2. Review `WORKSPACE.md` to understand the boundary scope of this project.

4. Place raw materials, logs, or unorganized information inside `_inbox/`.
5. Before running high-risk commands or modifying files, check `SAFEGUARDS.md`.
6. Use `MANIFEST.yaml` to understand the active workflow.

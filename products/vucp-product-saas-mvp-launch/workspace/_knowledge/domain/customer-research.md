# Customer Research

## The Mom Test Principles
1. **Talk about their life, not your idea.**
   - ❌ "Would you use an app that...?"
   - ✅ "How do you currently handle...?"

2. **Ask about specifics in the past, not generics in the future.**
   - ❌ "Would you pay for...?"
   - ✅ "When did you last pay for a solution to this?"

3. **Listen, don't pitch.** < 20% of time should be you talking.

## Finding Interviewees

### Where to look
- Reddit communities (r/startups, niche subreddits)
- Twitter/X — reply to complaints about the problem
- LinkedIn — cold outreach with specific ask
- Facebook groups
- Indie Hackers
- Your own network (start here!)

### Outreach Template
```
Subject: 10-minute question about [specific problem]

Hi [Name],

I saw your post about [specific problem they mentioned]. 
I'm researching how people handle this — not selling anything.

Would you have 10 minutes for a quick call? 
Happy to share what I learn afterward.

[Your name]
```

## Interview Structure (30 min)

1. **Context (2 min)**
   - "Tell me about your role and what you do daily."

2. **Problem exploration (15 min)**
   - "When did you last experience [problem]?"
   - "What did you do?"
   - "What tools did you try?"
   - "Why didn't they work perfectly?"
   - "What did that cost you?" (time, money, stress)

3. **Solution exploration (10 min)**
   - "If you had a magic wand, what would the solution look like?"
   - "What would you pay for that?" (only after they describe it)

4. **Close (3 min)**
   - "Can I follow up when I have something to show?"
   - "Do you know 2-3 others with this problem?"

## Red Flags (False Positives)
- "That sounds interesting" ≠ will buy
- "I would use that" ≠ will use
- "You should add X feature" ≠ they need X
- Compliments without specifics
- Future tense without past behavior

## Green Flags (Real Pain)
- They already pay for a partial solution
- They've tried multiple solutions
- They use workarounds (spreadsheets, manual processes)
- They can quantify the cost of the problem
- They introduce you to others with the same problem

## Documentation
After each interview, record:
- Date, name, role
- Top 3 quotes (verbatim)
- Pain level (1-10)
- Current solution
- Willingness to pay
- Referrals given

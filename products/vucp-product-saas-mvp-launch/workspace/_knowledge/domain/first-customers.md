# First Customers

## The 10-Customer Rule
Don't worry about scale until you have 10 people who:
1. Pay you money
2. Use the product regularly
3. Would be disappointed if you shut down

Everything before 10 is learning. Everything after 10 is optimization.

## Where to Find Them

### 1. Your Network (Easiest)
- People you interviewed during validation
- Friends who have the problem
- Former colleagues
- Twitter/LinkedIn connections

**Outreach:**
```
"Hey [Name],

Remember when you told me about [problem]? 
I built something that solves it.

Not ready for prime time, but would you 
want to try it? First month free, then $X.

[Your name]"
```

### 2. Communities (Medium)
- Where your target customers hang out
- Provide value first, sell second
- "I noticed many people struggle with X. I built Y."

### 3. Cold Outreach (Hardest)
- Only after you know exactly who to target
- Personalized, not templated
- Lead with value, not product

## The Sales Conversation

### Discovery Call (15 min)
1. "What does your current process look like?"
2. "Where does it break?"
3. "What have you tried?"
4. "What would ideal look like?"

### Demo (10 min)
- Show THEIR use case, not generic
- "Remember you said X? Here's how that works."
- Let them click, don't click for them

### Close (5 min)
- "Does this solve what we discussed?"
- "What would need to be true for you to start today?"
- "We can start with [small commitment]."

## Pricing First Customers

### The "Early Adopter" Deal
- 50% off for first 10 customers
- Lifetime discount for first 100
- "Founding customer" status
- Direct access to you

### Why discount?
- You're buying learning, not revenue
- Early customers are co-creators
- Their feedback is worth more than the discount
- They become case studies

### When to stop discounting?
- When you have 10 customers who'd pay full price
- When you have testimonials
- When you have product-market fit signals

## Signals of Product-Market Fit

### Weak Signals
- People sign up
- People use it once
- People say "this is cool"

### Strong Signals
- People use it 3+ times/week
- People invite teammates
- People ask for features (not just complain about bugs)
- People pay without you asking
- Churn < 5%/month
- NPS > 40

### The Ultimate Signal
"How would you feel if you could no longer use [Product]?"
- "Very disappointed" > 40% = product-market fit

## From 10 to 100

### Month 1-2: Manual
- Direct sales
- Personal outreach
- Community participation

### Month 3-6: Systematic
- Document what worked
- Create repeatable process
- Hire first sales/support

### Month 6-12: Scalable
- Content marketing
- Paid acquisition (if unit economics work)
- Partnerships
- Affiliate program

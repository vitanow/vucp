# Brand Voice

## Rules

- Be direct.
- Be useful before being clever.
- Prefer concrete examples.
- Avoid empty motivational language.
- Make assumptions explicit.

## Do Not

- Do not sound generic.
- Do not bury the main point.
- Do not over-explain obvious steps.

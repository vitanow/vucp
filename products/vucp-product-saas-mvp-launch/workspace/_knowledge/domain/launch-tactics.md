# Launch Tactics

## Launch Hierarchy

### 1. Silent Launch (Week -2)
- Product works end-to-end
- Payment processing active
- Analytics tracking
- 5-10 beta users using it

### 2. Friends & Family (Week -1)
- 10-20 people you know
- Ask for honest feedback, not compliments
- Fix critical bugs
- Gather 3 testimonials

### 3. Soft Launch (Week 0)
- Post in communities where you already participate
- NOT: spam every group
- YES: "I've been building X to solve Y. Would love feedback."

### 4. Public Launch (Week 1-2)
- Product Hunt
- Hacker News Show HN
- Reddit relevant communities
- Twitter thread
- LinkedIn post

### 5. Scale (Week 3+)
- Double down on what worked
- Kill what didn't
- Start paid channels if unit economics work

## Platform-Specific Tactics

### Product Hunt
- **Best day:** Tuesday or Wednesday
- **Best time:** 00:01 PST (be first)
- **Preparation:**
  - Gallery images (3-5, showing product)
  - Maker comment (your story, not features)
  - First commenter strategy (friends upvote early)
  - Respond to EVERY comment within first 2 hours

### Hacker News (Show HN)
- **Title format:** "Show HN: [Product] – [One-line benefit]"
- **Don't:** Sound corporate, use buzzwords
- **Do:** Show code, be technical, be honest about limitations
- **Best time:** Tuesday 8am PST

### Twitter/X
- **Format:** Thread (5-10 tweets)
- **Structure:**
  1. Hook (problem + pain)
  2. Journey (how you discovered it)
  3. Solution (what you built)
  4. Proof (screenshots, numbers)
  5. Ask (feedback, RT, try it)
- **Tag:** Relevant people (but don't spam)

### Reddit
- **Rule #1:** Be a member first, promoter second
- **Good communities:** r/SideProject, r/Entrepreneur, niche subs
- **Format:** "I built X because Y. Here's what I learned."
- **Never:** "Check out my new app!!!"

### Indie Hackers
- **Format:** Milestone post
- **Share:** Revenue numbers (even if $0), lessons, next steps
- **Community:** Comment on others' posts first

## Launch Day Checklist

- [ ] Product works on mobile
- [ ] Payment processing tested
- [ ] Analytics tracking active
- [ ] Support channel ready (email/Discord)
- [ ] Rollback plan documented
- [ ] Team knows their roles
- [ ] Scheduled posts ready
- [ ] Personal outreach list ready (50+ people)
- [ ] Crisis communication ready (if something breaks)

## Post-Launch (First 48 Hours)

### Hour 0-2
- Monitor analytics
- Respond to every comment/message
- Fix critical bugs immediately
- Document feedback

### Hour 2-24
- Analyze traffic sources
- Identify what resonated
- Follow up with interested people
- Update landing page based on feedback

### Day 2-7
- Send thank you to supporters
- Publish "lessons learned" post
- Start email sequence for signups
- Plan next feature based on feedback

## Common Launch Mistakes
- Launching before product works
- Launching on too many platforms at once
- Not having analytics (you won't know what worked)
- Ignoring negative feedback
- Celebrating too early (launch is start, not finish)
- Not following up with interested people

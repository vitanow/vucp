# Landing Page Psychology

## Core Principle
People don't buy products. They buy **better versions of themselves**.

## The 5-Second Rule
За 5 секунди посетителят трябва да разбере:
1. **What is this?**
2. **Is it for me?**
3. **Why should I care?**

## Structure

### 1. Hero Section
- **Headline:** Outcome, not feature. "Save 5 hours/week" not "Task automation tool"
- **Subheadline:** How it works in 1 sentence
- **CTA:** One action, low friction. "Start free" not "Sign up for enterprise trial"
- **Visual:** Product screenshot or result visualization

### 2. Problem Agitation
- "You know when..." (describe their pain vividly)
- "And then..." (escalate the pain)
- "So you end up..." (show the cost)

### 3. Solution
- "Here's the thing..." (introduce your approach)
- "Instead of X, you get Y" (contrast)
- 3-5 specific benefits (not features)

### 4. Social Proof
- "Used by [specific number] [specific people]"
- Quotes from beta users
- Logos if you have them
- "As seen on..." if applicable

### 5. How It Works
- 3 simple steps
- Each with icon + 1 sentence
- "And you're done."

### 6. FAQ
- 3-5 real objections
- Answer directly, no corporate speak
- Last question: "How do I get started?" → CTA

### 7. Final CTA
- Same as hero CTA
- Add urgency only if real ("First 100 users get X")

## Psychological Triggers

### Loss Aversion
- "Don't lose another hour to manual data entry"
- Not: "Save time" (gain) → "Stop wasting time" (loss)

### Specificity
- "Save 5.2 hours/week" > "Save time"
- "Used by 247 indie hackers" > "Used by many"
- "$49/month" > "Affordable pricing"

### Contrast
- Show before/after
- Show cost of problem vs. cost of solution
- "One coffee/day vs. 5 hours saved/week"

### Risk Reversal
- "14-day free trial, no credit card"
- "Cancel anytime"
- "Money-back guarantee"
- "Setup in 5 minutes, or we do it for you"

## Common Mistakes
- Talking about features, not outcomes
- Too much text above the fold
- Multiple CTAs competing
- No clear "who is this for"
- Generic testimonials ("Great product!")
- Hidden pricing
- No mobile optimization

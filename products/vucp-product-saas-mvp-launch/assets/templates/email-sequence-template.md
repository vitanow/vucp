# Email Sequence Template

## Email 1: Welcome (Immediate)
```
Subject: You're in! Here's what happens next.

Hi {{first_name}},

Thanks for joining the waitlist for {{product_name}}!

Here's what to expect:
- Launch date: {{launch_date}}
- First {{number}} get {{offer}}
- Weekly updates on our progress

Reply with your biggest {{problem}} challenge — 
I read every email.

{{sender_name}}
```

## Email 2: Progress Update (Week 1)
```
Subject: Week 1 update: {{milestone}}

Hi {{first_name}},

Quick update on {{product_name}}:

✅ {{milestone_1}}
🔄 {{milestone_2}}
📅 {{milestone_3}}

Behind the scenes: {{personal_story}}

Questions? Just reply.

{{sender_name}}
```

## Email 3: Launch Day
```
Subject: 🚀 {{product_name}} is live! (Waitlist discount inside)

Hi {{first_name}},

Today's the day. {{product_name}} is live.

As a waitlist member, you get:
✅ {{discount}} off first {{months}} months
✅ Priority support
✅ {{bonus}}

[Get Started — 2 Minute Setup]

This discount expires in {{hours}} hours.

{{sender_name}}
```

## Email 4: Post-Launch (Day 3)
```
Subject: How's your first week with {{product_name}}?

Hi {{first_name}},

Hope you're enjoying {{product_name}}!

Quick questions:
1. What's working well?
2. What's confusing?
3. What should we build next?

Reply directly — I read everything.

{{sender_name}}
```

## Email 5: Re-engagement (Day 14, if no activity)
```
Subject: Did something go wrong?

Hi {{first_name}},

I noticed you haven't tried {{product_name}} yet.

No pressure — but if something stopped you, I'd love to know.

Was it:
- Too complicated?
- Not what you expected?
- Bad timing?

Just reply with a number (1, 2, or 3). Or tell me more.

{{sender_name}}
```
